# Concept

Research one idea and leave the wiki with one canonical Concept page for it. Open each principle named at a step by reading `../<principle>/SKILL.md` before acting on that step; `principle-knowledge-notes`' Writing one page governs roles, lookup, title, form, entity links, the work note and the report.

## Step 1 — Resolve the vault roles and the target

Open `principle-knowledge-notes` and `principle-editing`, and resolve the roles and the Concept folder. Confirm by the principle's criteria, not the owner's label, that the idea is a Concept: a model, framework or method one author named, or a reusable idea, technique or pattern without an established field name. When it is a field term (`term`), a named individual (`entity`) or a solved procedure (a Guide), name that kind and why, and ask once before writing.

## Step 2 — Look the idea up in the vault

Look it up as the principle describes. Updating an existing page, open `principle-writing`.

## Step 3 — Research

Read the coiner's own account first, then the strongest independent treatments and the owner's notes that mention the idea, noting for each claim what its evidence does not establish. Open `principle-archival`.

## Step 4 — Write the page

Fill [concept](assets/concept.md). Open `principle-writing` and `principle-markdown`.

- `layer` is `concepts`, or `theory`, `method` or `scale` for a paper-derived Concept.
- Overview says what the idea offers the owner's work, in the page's own words.
- Details answers reader questions (how it works, why it holds, where it applies, where it stops), compiled from every source rather than walking one source in order.
- Terms links the field terms the idea uses; Related links neighbouring pages with the reason once.

Optional blocks, deleted unless they apply:

- The Contradiction callout only for an explicit conflict, as [updating](../ingest/references/updating.md) describes.
- The Bias Check for `confidence: high` or synthesis-heavy pages, with both lines filled.
- The scale block (Copyright caution through Use Cautions) and the `measuredConstruct` and `itemCount` keys only for `layer: scale`, when the paper develops or validates the scale.

## Step 5 — Link the work note

Link the concept from the work note as the principle describes. Open `principle-editing`.

## Step 6 — Read back and report

Read back and report as the principle describes, listing candidates before the footer.
