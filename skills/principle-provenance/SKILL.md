# Provenance

A record keeps its value as evidence only while its creator stays recoverable. A hosting site, a republisher or an aggregator is not the creator.

- Keep each record with its creator and the activity that produced it, and keep records of different creators distinct even when their subjects overlap.
- Identify the true creator behind a republisher or aggregator and record the original's locator.
- Keep a secondary article and the study it quotes as separate records with an explicit relationship; capture the study on its own only when it is itself selected.
- Attribute a secondary author's interpretation to that author.
- Report an unavailable original as a limitation; a quotation is not the original.

Example: a newsletter summary is a Raw capture that links the research report it summarizes by locator, and a Concept may synthesize both while naming which evidence supports each claim.

The vault's live policy overrides any line here. This principle shapes how a record is handled; it is not a reason to write, move or delete anything.
