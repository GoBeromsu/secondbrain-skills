# Preserve the author's voice

A note belongs to the person who wrote it, and a reader later trusts that its sentences are theirs.

- Line-edit prose, preserving the author's voice and meaning.
- Keep a sentence the owner wrote word for word when you revise the page around it; move it whole or leave it, never paraphrase it.
- Shape only prose the task asked for; this is not a reason to rewrite what it did not.

The vault's live policy overrides any line here. This principle shapes a write the task already asked for; it is not a reason to make one, and not a reason to withhold one.
