# Writing

A note is read later by someone who was not there when it was written, often its owner months on, and who trusts that its sentences are the evidence's and their author's.

## A note that stands alone

- Write the body as the canonical text for a reader who never saw the conversation: name the people who spoke or decided, and leave out the exchange that produced the note.
- Put uncertainty next to the content it qualifies ("amount unconfirmed"), once.
- Let the reader tell observation, assumption and interpretation apart.
- Prefer the primary source, and mark a claim whose source could not be checked as unchecked.

## The page is not a research log

The reader came for the subject and the owner's judgment. How the page was researched is the writer's process, it goes stale at once, and it buries the content, so it belongs in the report to the owner.

- Leave out which documents were read, at which commit or branch, and what was skipped, not run or not re-verified.
- Show sources as citations: frontmatter `source`, footnote locators and References.
- State a limit of the evidence as a fact about the evidence ("the maker's retrospective, not independent research"), not as what the writer did.
- Describe what a thing does rather than naming the source file that does it; a path or command belongs on the page only when the owner would type or open it themselves.

## The author's voice

The owner trusts that a sentence under their name is still theirs; a paraphrase quietly changes what they said.

- Line-edit prose, preserving the author's voice and meaning, and only prose the task asked for.
- Keep a sentence the owner wrote word for word when you revise the page around it: move it whole or leave it.

## Revise and consolidate

An existing page is the owner's accumulated record. An update makes it better without losing anything valid on it.

- Add only what is new: claims, sources and links the page does not already carry.
- Draft from the inspected evidence, not from the page's own prose, which would only echo what is already there.
- Change an existing statement only on verified evidence; when a source conflicts with it, keep both, attributed.

The step-by-step procedure (before-image, separate reviewer, template migration) is in [updating](../ingest/references/updating.md). The vault's live policy overrides any line here. This principle shapes a write the task already asked for; it is not a reason to make one, and not a reason to withhold one.
